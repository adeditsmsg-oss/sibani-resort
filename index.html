The Heritage Legacy

A Serene Sanctuary in the Heart of Kharagpur

Situated at the historic gateway of Chowrangee More in Kharagpur, Sibani Resort weaves together Bengal's rich culture of hospitality with an abundance of natural serenity.

What once served as a warm, convenient transit stop for travelers is now reimagined as a premium heritage eco-sanctuary. Surrounded by towering palms, manicured stone pathways, and traditional red-clay terracotta architecture, our resort offers a sensory escape from the hum of modern life.

Whether you are visiting for a peaceful weekend getaway, dining at our multi-cuisine culinary deck, or celebrating grand lifetime memories in our banquets, Sibani welcomes you with genuine care and timeless elegance.

Gastronomy & Tastes

A Journey of Bengal's Flavors

Food lies at the very heart of the Sibani experience. Our master chefs combine authentic, age-old recipes with local garden ingredients to curate a culinary palette that satisfies both travelers and food connoisseurs.

From rich, aromatic Bengali delicacies (like slow-cooked mutton *Kosha Mangsho*, mustard-laced *Hilsa*, and *Chingri Malaikari*) to hot-off-the-tandoor *Mughlai Kababs* and comforting *Indian-Chinese* favorites, every meal is prepared fresh with passion.

Choose to dine in our air-conditioned interior hall, or head up to **The Sky Deck**—our open-air dining space lit with warm fairy lights and custom hanging cane lanterns. It's the ultimate setting to watch sports or dine under the stars.

Regional Discovery

Explore the Kharagpur Region

Sibani Resort is situated at Chowrangee More, making it the ideal central base for academic visits to IIT Kharagpur, historic temple safaris, or forest trails.

12 mins away

IIT Kharagpur Campus

Visit India's first and largest Indian Institute of Technology. The campus features sprawling green walks, the Nehru Museum of Science and Technology, and historic heritage points.

20 mins away

Regional Terracotta Temples

Explore the ancient clay and terracotta temples scattered across the heritage areas. They showcase traditional hand-carved panels depicting mythological scenes from Bengal's history.

25 mins away

Gopegarh Heritage Eco-Park

Perched on the banks of the Kangsabati River, this scenic park houses historic fort ruins, nature watchtowers, and panoramic views of the river plains.

35 mins away

Salboni & Arabari Sal Forests

Drive out to the deep Sal and Eucalyptus woodlands. Home to traditional eco-tourism reserves, scenic drives, and seasonal elephant migration paths.
